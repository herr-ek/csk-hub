# Position appointments grant Group Membership

CSK Hub models a Position tenure once, in the Group that gives the responsibility its context, and lets that appointment provide dated Membership in every Group the Position entails (which may include the context, as for a Board office, and another Group, as for Sexmästare in the Board and Sexmästeriet). This preserves one source of truth for the holder and tenure while keeping distinct contexts—such as three Choir representatives—in a shared committee roster; separate holdings per Group would duplicate dates and make a handover or correction vulnerable to partial updates. This ADR is proposed for team discussion and is not an accepted decision until agreed.
