# Leaving a Group Conversation preserves earlier history

A User who leaves a Group Conversation retains read-only access to retained Messages
through their departure, but cannot read later Messages, send Messages, or subscribe
to new activity. Joining or rejoining grants access to all retained history. This
preserves the shared record a former member participated in, at the cost of leaving
being an end to future access rather than a revocation of all access. An active
member may add a former member again without an acceptance step; this restores
access to all retained history, including Messages sent during their absence.
